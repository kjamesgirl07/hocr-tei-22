The crew finished first in an official time of 13:58.9 and set a new course record for the men's Championship Eight. Harvard and Princeton were second and third, and Penn was fifth. The 1997 race was the 32nd Head of the Charles, because the 1996 regatta had been cancelled by a storm. The record stood for twenty years, until California rowed 13:27 in 2017.
CorrectionVersion 1.0 called the regatta a "32 year old race" and gave no time. Sources: HOCR course champions list (hocr.org), 1997 official results (hocr.org), and the Daily Pennsylvanian, October 1997.
