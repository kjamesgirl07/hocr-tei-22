The boat

row2k lists the crew from bow to stern. The entry was not the 1997 World Champion eight, but a boat drawn from members of the 1997 US Team. Cipollone uses surnames, nicknames ("Philthy", "Giboon") and seat numbers ("Seven, six and five, go!"), so the names in the transcript link here.

The rigging is assumed to be the standard US layout, with the stroke on port. The call "Richter … you drive the starboard side" is consistent with it.

The course

The Head of the Charles is a three-mile (4.8 km) head race rowed upstream on the Charles River between Boston and Cambridge. Crews start one at a time at intervals and race the clock. Winning depends as much on steering the long bends and the narrow bridge arches as on speed.

  1. Start, near the BU DeWolfe Boathouse · “Ready all, row!” US Rowing starts first.
  2. BU Bridge · Calls echo under the bridge. “We got flat water.”
  3. River Street Bridge · “Here's the first bridge, in the wind.” The one-minute commitment.
  4. Western Avenue Bridge · Crowds cheer. “They're cheering for you.” Taunting Richter about Princeton.
  5. Weeks Footbridge · Ahead: “The next turn will be to port, and it's the big dog.” (location not certain)
  6. Anderson Memorial Bridge
  7. Eliot Bridge
  8. Finish, near Christian Herter Park · “12:40 down, I think you can break the record!” Official time 13:58.9.

The excerpt names only the BU Bridge and the Western Avenue Bridge. The "first bridge" is identified as River Street from the order of the course, and this identification is marked as an editorial inference in the TEI.
